02 / CURATOR’S NOTE

Finding new echoes
in the textures
of history.

Music is where the exploration begins.

I study Music Technology and Arts at the Shanghai Conservatory of Music, exploring how sound tells stories and how interaction connects people with culture.

Archaeological sites, excavated artifacts and their histories are a particular interest of mine. I want to help visitors approach history through listening, touch and participation.

From interactive Unity exhibitions to Max/MSP sound experiments, I am developing a creative practice between music and technology.
